== (5) torsion is a cokernel ==
  coker(d_{d+1}) = C_d / im d_{d+1} forgets to intersect with ker d_d, so it is
  bigger than H_d -- but only by the free group im d_d.  Hence the torsion agrees
  and the free ranks differ by exactly rank d_d.
