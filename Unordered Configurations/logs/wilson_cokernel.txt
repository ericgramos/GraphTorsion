== the numerical fact behind the top-degree vanishing ==
   ker W_{n-1,n}(b) = 0   <=>   C(b,n-1) >= C(b,n)   <=>   b <= 2n-1

   rank marked * is computed over F_1000003 rather than by an exact SNF, which
   is safe here: rank mod p never exceeds the rational rank, and the diagonal
   entries of W_{n-1,n}(b) are all <= n (Wilson), so p cannot divide one.
